Frequently Asked Questions

How long does delivery take?
Delivery takes 2–3 business days inside Dhaka and 3–5 business days outside Dhaka (nationwide), via our courier partners.
What is your return policy?
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery, provided the item is unworn and in its original packaging.
Do you offer Cash on Delivery?
Yes, Cash on Delivery (COD) is available nationwide. You can also pay instantly using bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or a debit/credit card at checkout.
Which mobile banking (MFS) options do you support?
We accept bKash, Nagad and Rocket. Simply select your preferred option at checkout and confirm the payment from your MFS app or via USSD.
Do you deliver outside Dhaka?
Yes, we deliver nationwide across all districts of Bangladesh. Delivery charges are ৳70 inside Dhaka and ৳130 outside Dhaka, with free delivery on orders over ৳2,500.
How do I find my size?
Each product page includes a size guide link. If you're between sizes, we generally recommend sizing up.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Contact our support team as soon as possible after placing your order. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be changed.
Are the products on this site real inventory?
This site is a demonstration storefront. Product, order and customer data shown here is sample content for illustrating the shopping experience.
