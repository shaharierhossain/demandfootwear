About Demand Footwear

Step Into Your Demand.

Demand Footwear was founded in Dhaka on a simple idea: footwear should keep pace with your life, not slow it down. From city sneakers to hand-finished leather shoes, every pair we design balances comfort, durability and everyday style — made for the streets of Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet and beyond.

Our Story

What started as a small workshop in Dhaka focused on classic leather craftsmanship — drawing on Bangladesh's long-standing leather-making tradition — has grown into a full footwear house shipping nationwide, spanning sneakers, boots, sandals and accessories, without losing the attention to detail that got us here.

Our Promise

Every pair is quality-checked before it leaves our warehouse. We stand behind our products with a straightforward returns policy, Cash on Delivery across Bangladesh, and a support team that actually answers.

Sustainability

We're continually evaluating our materials and packaging to reduce our footprint — from responsibly sourced leather to recyclable shipping materials.
